# Source-to-review rules for allene chemistry

Apply the general rule pack first. This file adds the distinctions needed for allenation, allene synthesis, propargylic chemistry, and axial stereocontrol.

## Evidence record

For each paper, capture:

```text
starting-material class
allene product topology and substitution
bond-forming or isomerization event
catalyst, ligand, promoter, and their loadings
conditions that determine the outcome
yield, conversion, regioselectivity, dr/de, ee/er, and their measurement basis
scope classes and explicit failures
mechanistic evidence and author interpretation
operational or scale evidence
role in the review argument
```

Scientific facts come from the current source set and evidence anchors. Style rules supply organization only.

## Paragraph logic

Choose the relationship before writing:

### Gap to solution

State the inaccessible product, substrate, or stereochemical class, then the design change that enabled it and the boundary that remains.

```text
Earlier systems covered [class A]. [New catalyst or substrate design] extended the reaction to [class B], although [remaining boundary].
```

### Improvement with tradeoff

Attach benefit and cost to the same protocol.

```text
[Modified conditions] improved [yield/selectivity/scope/operation] relative to [baseline], while [cost or weak class] remained.
```

### Platform and extensions

Describe the platform once. Group follow-up work by the product family, substrate class, stereocontrol problem, or practical limitation each one changes.

### Scope and boundary

Name compatible classes and the strongest observed failures. Keep untested classes separate from tested failures.

### Mechanism and evidence

State the observation first, then the supported interpretation. Separate product evidence, intermediate observation, labeling, trapping, kinetics, spectroscopy, computation, and author proposal.

### Practical repair

When a later method lowers loading, changes scale, removes a work-up burden, or improves reproducibility, state the operational change with its demonstrated substrate range and remaining cost.

### Reproducibility correction

Describe the original protocol, the observed reliability problem, the revised condition, and the resulting outcome in neutral terms.

### Application

Connect an allene-building method to the downstream transformation or target. Keep upstream method performance separate from the value of the application.

## Platform-specific comparisons

Compare methods on shared axes:

```text
precursor availability
allene substitution pattern
new bond formed
catalyst and activation mode
required protecting or directing group
regio- and stereocontrol
functional-group and substrate range
operation, scale, and loading
mechanism evidence
failure classes
```

For ATA/EATA development lines, keep aldehyde and ketone partners, terminal and internal alkynes, racemic and asymmetric variants, and stoichiometric and catalytic protocols distinct. For propargylic substitution, separate electrophile class, nucleophile class, metal/ligand system, and allene product topology.

## Claim calibration

- `Catalyzed` describes a catalyst used below stoichiometric loading; report high or near-stoichiometric metal use as promoted or mediated when appropriate.
- A proposed catalytic cycle establishes a hypothesis. Controls, intermediates, kinetics, spectroscopy, labeling, and computation determine how strongly it can be stated.
- `Broad scope` is replaced by the demonstrated substrate classes and named weak or failed classes.
- A maximum ee or yield is one example; summarize the main range and outliers.
- Match each metric to the correct product, regioisomer, diastereomer, recovered substrate, or transformed fraction.
- Optical rotation, indirect transfer, NMR, and chiral chromatography provide different analytical confidence; name the method when it affects the claim.
- “First” and field-wide absence require an adequately checked literature range.

## Citation behavior

Place the citation beside the clause it supports. When one paper supports conditions and another supports mechanism or historical priority, split the claims. Use review articles for orientation and primary papers for experimental details and high-risk claims.

## Compact paragraph pattern

```text
[Transformation and contribution]. [Representative conditions, outcome, and scope]. [Comparison or mechanism evidence]. [Boundary and significance for the section].
```

Use another structure when the evidence relationship calls for it; the paragraph's job is synthesis, not completion of a template.
