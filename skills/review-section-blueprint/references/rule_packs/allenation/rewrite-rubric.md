# Allene review rewrite rubric

## Source inspection

Use the paper sections best suited to each field:

| Information | Primary source location |
|---|---|
| Identity and headline contribution | title, abstract, graphical abstract |
| Catalyst and conditions | optimization table, scheme footnotes, experimental section |
| Product topology and scope | scope schemes and captions |
| Weak or failed classes | table entries, footnotes, discussion, supporting information |
| Selectivity and analytical basis | schemes, footnotes, analytical methods |
| Mechanism | controls, labeling, trapping, kinetics, spectroscopy, computation |
| Scale and operation | procedure, scale-up experiment, work-up description |

## Extraction record

Before drafting, record:

```text
paper role
precursor and product classes
reaction identity
catalyst/ligand/promoter and loading
decisive conditions
representative outcomes
scope classes
weak and failed classes
selectivity type and measurement
mechanistic observations and interpretation
scale or practical evidence
comparison baseline
```

## Evidence strength

Use the strongest accurate verb:

| Evidence | Wording |
|---|---|
| Isolated or directly measured result | observed, afforded, reached, showed |
| Directly observed intermediate or species | detected, characterized, observed |
| Controls, labeling, trapping, kinetics, or computation | supports, indicates, is consistent with |
| Author explanation without discriminating evidence | proposes, attributes, suggests |
| Review synthesis across checked papers | taken together, within this corpus, these studies indicate |

## Chemistry checks

- Reaction class, starting material, product topology, and allene substitution are correct.
- Catalyst, ligand, promoter, reagent, and stoichiometric roles are separated.
- Yield, conversion, rr, dr/de, ee/er, and optical purity are not merged.
- Each metric is attached to the right product and analytical method.
- Scope uses classes and distributions, with important outliers retained.
- Untested, failed, low-yielding, and low-selectivity classes remain distinct.
- Mechanistic certainty matches the source evidence.
- Catalyst-controlled stereochemistry is separated from substrate chirality transfer.

## Synthesis checks

- The opening states the paragraph's review judgment.
- Experimental detail is selected for comparison, scope, mechanism, or practical use.
- The baseline is named when improvement is claimed.
- Complementary methods remain complementary; one is called a replacement only when the evidence supports that judgment.
- Platform development is summarized once, with follow-up papers contributing only their material changes.
- The closing sentence states the boundary, consequence, or transition.

## Style checks

- Concrete chemistry replaces generic praise.
- Qualifications sit beside the claim they limit.
- Repeated caveats are removed after the claim is bounded once.
- Author chronology is used only when development order matters.
- Sentence length reflects one main chemical or argumentative relationship.

## Ready state

A paragraph is ready when the transformation is chemically identifiable, the important numbers are traceable, the comparison is fair, the mechanism is calibrated, and the reader can see why the paper matters.
